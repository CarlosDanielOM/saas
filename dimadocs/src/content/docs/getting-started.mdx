This guide gets DomDimaBot working in your Twitch chat. You need the Twitch account that owns the channel and about 10 minutes. New accounts start on the **Free** plan, which is enough for this setup.

Only the channel owner can turn the bot on or off or activate it. Helpers can use only the pages the owner gave them.

<Steps>

1. **Log in with Twitch**

   Go to [domdimabot.com](https://domdimabot.com) and click **Login with Twitch**. Sign in with the Twitch account that owns the channel. Twitch asks you to approve; approve it, and you return to the dashboard.

   You are done when the dashboard opens.

2. **Turn the bot on**

   In the dashboard header, click **Turn the bot on**. The bot joins your chat, and the header shows **Bot in your chat**.

   If the button says **Allow on Twitch** instead, click it. Twitch asks you to sign in again, and the bot joins your chat right after. This can happen on accounts made before this button existed.

   To take the bot out of your chat, click **Turn the bot off**, then click again when it says **Tap again to turn off**.

   You are done when the dashboard header shows **Bot in your chat**.

3. **Add your first command**

   Some built-in commands already work, such as `!commands` and `!s` (text to speech). `!s` reads a message aloud once the speech overlay is in OBS; see [Text to Speech](/tts/).

   To make your own, open **Commands** and click **New command**. Enter a name such as `hello`, a response, and save. Viewers then type `!hello` in your chat.

   You are done when your test command replies in chat. If it does not, check that the dashboard header shows **Bot in your chat** and the command is enabled, then wait for its cooldown to end.

   [![The new command dialog with a hello command and sample reply](/guides/commands/commands-create-dark.png)](/guides/commands/commands-create-dark.png)

   *Example data for DemoStreamer; the dialog shows where to enter a command name and reply.*

4. **Activate Bot (optional)**

   Your own commands, text to speech, Triggers, Overlay Studio and more work without this step. Click **Activate Bot** when you want the rest. You find it in the top bar, on the dashboard **Get started** card, and next to the features that need it. Twitch asks you to approve, and you return to the dashboard.

   Activating the bot turns on:

   - More built-in commands, such as `!so`, `!poll`, `!title`, `!game`, `!followage`, and the mod and VIP tools.
   - Chat events and alerts for follows, subs, Bits, raids, and going live.
   - Automod and follow-bot protection.
   - Channel point rewards.
   - Polls and predictions in overlays.
   - Twitch goals on the dashboard.
   - Follower and sub stats.

   Until then, those features show **Needs full access**.

5. **Look at modules (optional)**

   Open **Modules** to see the features you can turn on, such as Text to Speech, Triggers, Chat Moderation, Clips, and Analytics. The catalog identifies each module’s plan and maturity; stable items have no status badge. For the channel owner, a locked card shows **Upgrade to Access**.

   Some Alpha tools need a closer look before you rely on them. **Overlay Studio** is available on Free and only the channel owner can open it. **Roulette** is available on Free and can be opened by the channel owner or a helper with full access.

</Steps>

## Permission buttons in the top bar

| Button | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Activate Bot** | The bot does not have full access yet. Your own commands still work. | Click it when you want the features that need full access, and approve on Twitch. |
| **Reauthenticate** | You activated the bot, but it no longer has the Twitch access it needs. | Click it and approve again. |
| **Update Permissions** | You activated the bot, and it now needs permissions it did not have before. | Click it and approve the new list. |

If a button appears again after you approved, reload the page and check that you are signed in with the channel owner's Twitch account. The dashboard stays usable while the button is showing.

## Check that setup worked

- The dashboard header shows **Bot in your chat**.
- Your test command replies in chat.

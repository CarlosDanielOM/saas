# Sound licenses

Every audio file in this folder (except `tts/`, whose clips are logged in `tts/index.json` and whose voices are listed
in `../voices.json`) must have a line here before a scene can use it. Promo videos are ads: the license must allow
commercial use.

| File | Source | License | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|

Kit sounds (`kit:pop`, `kit:ding`, `kit:chime`, `kit:thud`, `kit:whoosh`, `kit:sparkle`) are generated from scratch by
`../lib/sfx.mjs` and need no entry.

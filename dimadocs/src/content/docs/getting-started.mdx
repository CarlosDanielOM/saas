This guide gets DomDimaBot working in your Twitch chat. You need the Twitch account that owns the channel and about 10 minutes. New accounts start on the **Free** plan, which is enough for this setup.

Only the channel owner can add the bot to chat or give it full access. Helpers can use only the pages the owner gave them.

<Steps>

1. **Log in with Twitch**

   Go to [domdimabot.com](https://domdimabot.com) and click **Login with Twitch**. Sign in with the Twitch account that owns the channel. Twitch asks you to approve; approve it, and you return to the dashboard.

   You are done when the dashboard opens.

2. **Add the bot to your chat**

   In the top bar, click **Add bot to chat**. The button changes to **Bot in chat · Remove**, and the dashboard header shows **Bot in your chat**.

   If the button says **Allow on Twitch** instead, click it. Twitch asks you to approve, and the bot joins your chat right after. This can happen on accounts made before this button existed.

   To take the bot out of your chat, click **Remove**, then click again when it says **Tap again to remove**.

   You are done when the dashboard header shows **Bot in your chat**.

3. **Add your first command**

   Open **Commands** and click **New command**. Enter a name such as `hello`, a response, and save. Viewers then type `!hello` in your chat.

   For a text-to-speech command, click **Text-to-speech command** on the **Commands** page. It opens the dialog with `!tts` and this reply filled in; save it:

   ```
   $(tts $(user) says: &t)
   ```

   Viewers then type `!tts` and a message, and it is read aloud on stream. Speech plays only while the speech overlay is in OBS; see [Text to Speech](/tts/).

   You are done when your test command replies in chat. If it does not, check that the top bar shows **Bot in chat** and the command is enabled, then wait for its cooldown to end.

   [![The new command dialog with a hello command and sample reply](/guides/commands/commands-create-dark.png)](/guides/commands/commands-create-dark.png)

   *Example data for DemoStreamer; the dialog shows where to enter a command name and reply.*

4. **Give full access (optional)**

   Your own commands and text to speech work without this step. Click **Give full access** when you want the rest. You find it in the profile menu (your avatar on a computer, the ☰ menu on a phone), on the dashboard **Get started** card, and next to the features that need it. Twitch asks you to approve, and you return to the dashboard.

   Full access turns on:

   - Built-in commands, such as `!so`, `!poll`, `!title`, `!game`, `!clip`, and the mod and VIP tools.
   - Chat events and alerts for follows, subs, Bits, raids, and going live.
   - Automod and follow-bot protection.
   - Channel point rewards.
   - Polls and predictions in overlays.
   - Twitch goals on the dashboard.
   - Follower and sub stats.

   Until then, those features show **Needs full access**.

5. **Look at modules (optional)**

   Open **Modules** to see the features you can turn on, such as Text to Speech, Triggers, Chat Moderation, Clips, and Analytics. The catalog identifies each module’s plan and maturity; stable items have no status badge. For the channel owner, a locked card shows **Upgrade to Access**.

   Some Alpha tools need a closer look before you rely on them. **Overlay Studio** is available on Free and only the channel owner can open it. **Roulette** is available on Free and can be opened by the channel owner or a helper with full access.

</Steps>

## Permission buttons in the top bar

| Button | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Add bot to chat** | The bot is not in your chat. | Click it to add the bot. |
| **Bot in chat · Remove** | The bot is in your chat. | Nothing. To take it out, click **Remove** and click again to confirm. |
| **Allow on Twitch** | Twitch needs your OK before the bot can join your chat. | Click it and approve on Twitch. The bot joins right after. |
| **Reauthenticate** | You gave full access, but the bot no longer has the Twitch access it needs. | Click it and approve again. |
| **Update Permissions** | You gave full access, and the bot now needs permissions it did not have before. | Click it and approve the new list. |

**Give full access** is not in the top bar. Find it in the profile menu, on the dashboard **Get started** card, or next to a feature that needs it.

If a button appears again after you approved, reload the page and check that you are signed in with the channel owner's Twitch account. The dashboard stays usable while the button is showing.

## Check that setup worked

- The top bar shows **Bot in chat · Remove**.
- The dashboard header shows **Bot in your chat**.
- Your test command replies in chat.

The **Analytics** hub groups your channel's audience tools in one place. It has one working tool today, **Follow Ledger**, which lists everyone who follows your channel. The hub and Follow Ledger need a **Premium** or **Pro** plan.

**Who can open it:** the channel owner, or an admin with the **Analytics** view permission.

The hub is marked **Beta**. It also lists planned tools, such as chat activity and stream retention. Those are not available yet.

## Open Follow Ledger

1. Open **Modules → Analytics**. The hub shows your plan status and the Follow Ledger card.
2. Select **Open Follow Ledger**.

On a Free channel, the card and page show a locked notice. Owners see an **Upgrade to unlock** button there.

## What Follow Ledger shows

- **Current followers:** the total of people who follow your channel right now.
- **Mutual followers:** people you also follow back.
- **Following you only:** people who follow you but whom you don't follow back.

Select a total to filter the list. Then:

- **Search** by follower name, login, or ID.
- **Sort** by follow date, **Newest** or **Oldest**.
- Each row shows the follow date and how long ago it happened.

Only current followers appear. People who have unfollowed are stored, but the dashboard doesn't show them.

## How often it updates

- The bot syncs your follower list from Twitch **once a day, at midnight UTC**.
- The **Refresh** button reloads the saved list. It doesn't fetch new data from Twitch, so a new follower may not appear until the next daily sync.

## Stream history on your dashboard

Your main dashboard shows **Last 30 Days Stream History**, with average viewers, new followers, and Bits for the last 30 days. This summary always covers 30 days, whatever your plan.

## Data retention

The [Plans](/plans/) page lists stream analytics retention of **30 days on Free, 180 days on Premium, and 365 days on Pro**. Today the system deletes stored viewer snapshots older than **90 days** for every plan, so don't count on the longer periods for viewer data.

Follow Ledger doesn't publish a retention period.

## Troubleshooting

- **The hub or Follow Ledger is locked:** the channel needs Premium or Pro.
- **You can't open the page:** you need to be the channel owner or have the **Analytics** view permission. Your plan doesn't replace this access.
- **Follow Ledger shows an error:** select **Refresh** and try again. If it keeps failing, check that the channel's Twitch authorization is current.

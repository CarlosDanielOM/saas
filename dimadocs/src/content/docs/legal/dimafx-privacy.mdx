**Effective November 4, 2026**

This policy describes how DimaFX, the Twitch Extension shop and its related playback, inventory, and credit features, handles information. DimaFX is provided by Carlos D. Ordonez, an individual operating DomDimaBot under the CDOM Labs name, in Arizona, United States. CDOM Labs is a business name, not a separate incorporated company.

## Information DimaFX handles

Depending on how you use DimaFX, it may handle:

- **Twitch and channel information:** Twitch may send technical extension information such as an opaque ID, channel ID, or authorization token for the extension to operate, even if you have not chosen to share your identity with DimaFX. Separately, if you authorize identity sharing, DimaFX receives your Twitch user ID to associate eligible activity with you and the right channel.
- **Shop and account records:** broadcaster-created items and settings, viewer inventory, channel credits, and saved viewer preferences.
- **Transaction and playback records:** transaction identifiers and details needed to fulfill a request, prevent duplicate fulfillment, issue eligible credits, provide support, and keep service records.
- **Content:** item names, descriptions, media references, fixed TTS text, and text entered by viewers. Viewer text may be associated with an identified transaction and playback record.
- **Operational and support information:** technical events and request information, which may include IP address, browser or device details, and request data, as handled by the service and its providers for operation, analytics, error tracking, security, troubleshooting, and product improvement. If you contact support, we handle the information and correspondence you send.

Some preset content may be used without identifying a viewer. Sharing your Twitch user ID with DimaFX is optional for features that do not need saved inventory, credits, preferences, or viewer-entered text. These identity-based features, including custom viewer-written TTS text, require that shared ID so the service can associate the information with your transaction and playback. Fixed TTS text selected by a broadcaster does not require viewer identity. If you stop sharing or withdraw authorization where Twitch provides that control, it does not by itself delete records already created; you may contact support to request deletion. Twitch extension authorization does not ask you for a card or password. Twitch also handles information under its own notices and privacy policy.

## How information is used

Information is used to configure and display the shop, connect a viewer's activity to a channel, process requests, play media or speech, maintain inventory and credits, prevent duplicate or abusive transactions, provide support, protect the service, troubleshoot errors, understand product use, and improve DimaFX. Depending on applicable law and the activity, processing is based on providing the features you request, our legitimate interests in operating, securing, and improving the service, your consent where we ask for it, or compliance with legal obligations. Internal analytics and error tracking support these purposes. We do not sell or rent personal information, use or share it for advertising or marketing, or monetize it.

## When information is shared

Information may be shared as needed to run DimaFX:

- With Twitch, through the extension and the platform interactions needed for authorization and Bits-enabled features.
- With the broadcaster's stream overlay so configured media or speech can play. Broadcasters and viewers may then capture or publish that output in streams, VODs, clips, or other recordings.
- With hosting, network, and storage providers that support the service.
- When necessary to protect users or the service, comply with law, respond to valid legal process, or address abuse and security incidents.

The service uses Google Fonts for the documentation and interface typography. DimaFX text-to-speech uses Piper hosted by the service; it does not require sending viewer text to a third-party speech provider. Primary hosting is Hetzner HEL1 in Helsinki, Finland (EU). Administration or support may involve access from the United States, and providers may process information in other locations as part of their services.

## Public playback and viewer text

Custom text submitted by an identified viewer is retained with related transaction and playback records. If it is used for TTS, it can be spoken in a public stream and may remain in the broadcaster's VOD, clips, or other recordings. DimaFX cannot remove copies made or published by broadcasters or other viewers. Do not submit sensitive information or text you do not want heard or recorded.

## Retention and deletion

DimaFX transaction and playback log records expire 365 days after creation. This period applies to those logs; it does not mean that every DimaFX record automatically expires after 365 days. Shop configuration remains until the broadcaster removes it. Necessary saved inventory, channel credits, and preferences remain while needed to provide those features and until deleted or no longer needed for their ongoing operation.

When a broadcaster deletes their DomDimaBot channel account, DimaFX deletes the channel's shop items, viewer wallets and saved inventory, transaction logs, and playback records. To make a privacy request, email [support@domdimabot.com](mailto:support@domdimabot.com). We may ask for reasonable information to verify your request and protect the account. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or portability; to restrict or object to processing; to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; or to complain to a data protection authority. We will respond to valid requests as required by law. Requests to withdraw consent do not affect processing already carried out before withdrawal.

## Children and security

DimaFX is not intended for children under 13, or anyone below a higher minimum age required by Twitch or local law. Do not use it if you do not meet the applicable minimum age. We use reasonable measures intended to protect information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy as DimaFX changes. Material updates will be communicated through the service or another reasonable channel, and the effective date will be updated. For privacy questions or requests, contact [support@domdimabot.com](mailto:support@domdimabot.com).

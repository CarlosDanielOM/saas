DomDimaBot connects to your Twitch channel through the dashboard. Signing in, authorizing channel permissions, and joining chat are separate steps.

<Steps>

1. **Sign in with Twitch**

   Open [domdimabot.com](https://domdimabot.com) and choose **Login with Twitch**. A new account starts on the Free plan. Your subscription plan and Twitch permissions are separate: upgrading a plan does not grant Twitch permissions.

2. **Activate and authorize your channel**

   In the dashboard header, use **Activate Bot**, **Reauthenticate**, or **Update Permissions** if that button appears. Twitch will show the permissions being requested. Return to the dashboard after authorizing.

   <Aside type="note">
   If Twitch permissions expire or change, the header can ask you to authorize again. The dashboard may still be accessible while a bot feature needs renewed permission.
   </Aside>

3. **Join chat**

   On your channel dashboard, choose **Join Bot**. The status changes when chat is enabled. You can use **Leave Chat** later to turn chat participation off.

4. **Set up a command or module**

   Open **Commands** to manage chat commands. Open **Modules** to find features such as TTS, triggers, chat moderation, and channel rewards. Availability depends on the module's status, your plan, and your channel permissions.

</Steps>

Stream Summaries lists recorded broadcast sessions with their metrics, an AI-generated recap and highlights when available, and proposed or applied memory actions. It is available in the dashboard under **Modules → Stream Summaries**. The module is listed as stable and has no minimum plan tier. AI generation uses the channel's AI credit allowance; the exact cost depends on provider usage and is not shown as a fixed per-summary price.

## Enable post-stream summaries

1. Connect your Twitch channel and open **Modules → AI Personality**.
2. Turn on **Stream summaries**.
3. Under **Learning config**, turn on **Create post-stream summaries** and save the settings.
4. Let the bot observe a broadcast, then open **Modules → Stream Summaries** to review results.

The separate **AI chat responses** switch controls chat replies; it does not control stream summaries. The learning pipeline's general **Enable learning pipeline** switch controls weekly and monthly maintenance jobs, but post-stream summaries use the two switches above.

By default, a stream-offline summary requires a session of at least 20 minutes and at least 30 sampled chat messages. You can change both thresholds in Learning config. A session below either threshold is recorded as **No-op** and does not produce a recap or memory actions. The service needs the stream session, viewer snapshots, and retained chat context to build a useful result.

## Read a summary

Select a session in the list to see its duration, average and peak viewers, sampled chat count, follows, subscriptions, Bits, and donations. The **Recap** tab shows the generated headline, recap, and highlights. The **Memories** tab shows proposed actions and, where available, their evidence, confidence, risk, and whether they were applied or skipped.

Memory changes follow the confirmation and auto-apply settings in **AI Personality → Learning config**. Review those settings and the memory policy before enabling automatic changes. To review or manage retained memories directly, open the dashboard's **Memories** module.

## Status and limitations

- **Pending** means processing has not completed.
- **Applied** means at least one memory action was applied; it does not guarantee every proposal was applied.
- **No-op** means no action was applied, for example because the session did not meet its thresholds or the model proposed no applicable change.
- **Failed** means the processing run did not complete successfully.

The recap and memory suggestions are model output based on sampled chat and available session data. They can omit events, misread context, or contain errors; treat them as aids for review rather than a complete or authoritative transcript. Processing can also be delayed or unavailable when required data or an AI provider is unavailable. A stream-offline summary email is sent only when generation succeeds and the account has an active email destination.

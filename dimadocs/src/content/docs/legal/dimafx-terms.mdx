## Using the shop and playback

Broadcasters configure which items are offered, their availability, and their content. DimaFX may let you activate or unlock an item through Twitch's Bits feature, or use channel-scoped DimaFX credits where available. Twitch controls its Bits feature and related transactions. Credits are an internal feature of one channel: they are not Bits, money, a deposit, or a transferable balance, and cannot be exchanged for cash. Credits can be used only for eligible items in the channel that issued them.

Playback depends on the broadcaster's settings, a connected stream overlay, the playback queue, Twitch, and network availability. Items may be unavailable, delayed, declined, interrupted, or fail to play. The queue holds up to 25 waiting plays. DimaFX may record an attempted or completed transaction to prevent duplicate fulfillment and to operate the service. If a play activated with Bits cannot be delivered because the overlay is closed, playback fails or cannot be confirmed, the item changes or is removed before playback, the queue is full, or a viewer-written message is refused after Twitch confirms the Bits, DimaFX issues channel credits to the Twitch account that used the Bits, equal to the Bits amount. Twitch provides the payer's account ID with the transaction, so sharing a Twitch ID with DimaFX is not required for those credits to be issued; the viewer can see them after sharing their ID. These credits are the make-good for an undelivered play, not Bits or money, and cannot be cashed out or transferred. They do not promise a refund of a Twitch transaction. Rights or remedies that cannot legally be waived remain available.

Broadcasters may change, disable, or remove items, including items saved in viewer inventory. Removing an item can make its saved copies unusable. A broadcaster may choose **Delete and refund saved copies**, which converts each saved copy into channel credits at the amount that viewer used. Broadcasters can skip a play in progress or remove a waiting play from the queue; plays they remove do not return credits to the viewer. Credits cannot be exchanged for cash or Bits. This does not limit any remedy required by law.

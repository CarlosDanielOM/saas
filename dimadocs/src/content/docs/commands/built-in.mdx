Built-in commands are ready-made tools that come with DomDimaBot. Viewers and moderators type them in chat. Each one has a default **Who can use it** level and a cooldown, and you can change both.

## Turn them on or change access

- **On the dashboard:** open **Commands**, find the **Built-in** section, and use the **On for chat** switch. Open a command to change **Who can use it**.
- **In chat:** moderators can use `!enable <name>` and `!disable <name>`. The name works with or without `!`.
- **Built-in commands can't be deleted.**
- **Cooldowns:** most built-in commands wait 10 seconds between uses. The wait is shared by the whole channel. `!s` and `!vanish` have no wait.

New channels start with `!s` on for everyone and `!clearchat` off. Existing channels keep their saved settings.

In the tables below, **Everyone** means anyone in chat, **Mods and above** includes mods, editors, admins, and you, and **Editors and above** includes editors, admins, and you.

## Info

| Command | Usage | Who can use it | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `!commands` | `!commands` | Everyone | Lists the commands the viewer can use. Keywords and repeating messages are not included. |
| `!followage` | `!followage [user]` | Everyone | Shows how long the viewer, or the user you name, has followed the channel, in years, months, days, and hours. |

## Fun and games

| Command | Usage | Who can use it | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `!sumimetro` | `!sumimetro [user]` | Everyone | Gives a random submissive and dominant reading. Each viewer gets one reading, remembered for 20 hours. Add a name to see that person's saved reading. |
| `!amor` | `!amor <user>` or `!amor <user1> <user2>` | Everyone | Gives a random love percentage. With one name, it compares the viewer and that person. |
| `!memide` | `!memide` | Everyone | Gives a random joke measurement. |
| `!ponerla` | `!ponerla` | Everyone | Gives a random joke percentage. |
| `!mecabe` | `!mecabe` | Everyone | Gives a random joke measurement. |
| `!ruletarusa` | `!ruletarusa` | Everyone | Pulls the trigger. Details are below. |
| `!duel` | `!duel <user>`, then `!duel accept` or `!duel decline` | Everyone | Challenges a viewer to a duel. Details are below. |
| `!vanish` | `!vanish` | Everyone | Times the viewer out for 3 seconds, so their messages disappear. Mods lose mod status for 10 seconds and get it back. Editors can't use it. |

## Text to speech

| Command | Usage | Who can use it | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `!s` | `!s <message>` | Everyone | Reads the message aloud with text to speech. Uses TTS credits. The reply is an editable template, `$(tts $(user) dice: &t)` by default. See [Text to speech](/tts/). |

## Stream and shoutouts

| Command | Usage | Who can use it | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `!clip` | `!clip [seconds] [title]` | Everyone | Creates a clip and posts its link. A first number from 5 to 60 sets the length. Any other first word starts the title. Numbers outside 5–60 use 30 seconds. |
| `!so` | `!so <user>` | Mods and above | Posts a shoutout announcement with the channel link and last game. Sends Twitch's own shoutout, and shows one of their clips when available. The same person gets a Twitch shoutout at most once every 2 minutes. |
| `!promo` | `!promo <user>` | Mods and above | Posts a chat message with the channel's name, game, title, and link. Use it when mobile viewers should see the shoutout. |
| `!anuncio` | `!anuncio <message>` | Mods and above | Posts a Twitch announcement. The message can be up to 300 characters. |
| `!game` | `!game <name>` | Mods and above | Changes the stream category. If no category matches the name exactly, the bot uses the top search result. Without permission, or with no name, it replies with the current category. |
| `!title` | `!title <title>` | Mods and above | Changes the stream title. Without permission, or with no title, it replies with the current title. |
| `!onlyemotes` | `!onlyemotes [seconds]` | Mods and above | Turns emote-only chat on or off. Each use switches the mode. Add seconds to turn it on for that long. |
| `!clearchat` | `!clearchat` | Mods and above (off by default) | Clears the chat. |

## Polls and predictions

| Command | Usage | Who can use it | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `!poll` | `!poll <title>;<option1>/<option2>;<seconds>` | Mods and above | Starts a Twitch poll. |
| `!endpoll` | `!endpoll` | Mods and above | Ends the active poll. |
| `!cancelpoll` | `!cancelpoll` | Mods and above | Cancels the active poll. |
| `!predi` | `!predi <title>;<outcome1>/<outcome2>;<seconds>` | Mods and above | Starts a Twitch prediction. |
| `!lockpredi` | `!lockpredi` | Mods and above | Closes betting on the active prediction. |
| `!endpredi` | `!endpredi <number>` | Mods and above | Ends the prediction and picks the winner by number. `1` is the first outcome. |
| `!cancelpredi` | `!cancelpredi` | Mods and above | Cancels an active or locked prediction. |

## Mods and VIPs

| Command | Usage | Who can use it | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `!mod` | `!mod <user>` | Editors and above | Adds a moderator. |
| `!unmod` | `!unmod <user>` | Editors and above | Removes a moderator. |
| `!vip` | `!vip <user> [days]` | Editors and above | Adds a VIP. With days (`1`–`365`), the VIP status ends after that many days. Without days, it stays until removed. |
| `!unvip` | `!unvip <user>` | Editors and above | Removes a VIP. |

## Manage commands from chat

| Command | Usage | Who can use it | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `!cc` | `!cc <name> <reply>` | Mods and above | Creates a custom command. See [Custom commands](/commands/overview/). |
| `!ec` | `!ec <name> <reply>` | Mods and above | Edits a custom command's reply, cooldown, or access. |
| `!dc` | `!dc <name>` | Mods and above | Deletes a custom command. |
| `!enable` | `!enable <name>` | Mods and above | Turns a command on. |
| `!disable` | `!disable <name>` | Mods and above | Turns a command off. |
| `!cct` | `!cct <name> <minutes> <message>` | Mods and above | Creates a repeating message. See [Custom commands](/commands/overview/). |
| `!ect` | `!ect <name> [minutes] [message]` | Mods and above | Edits a repeating message. |
| `!dct` | `!dct <name>` | Mods and above | Deletes a repeating message. |
| `!timer` | `!timer create`, `edit`, `delete`, or `list` | Mods and above | Does the same work as `!cct`, `!ect`, and `!dct` in one command. `!timers` also works. |

## Details

### Ruletarusa

- Each pull has a 1-in-3 chance of firing.
- A loss gives a timeout of 10 seconds. Each following loss adds 10 seconds: 20, then 30, and so on. The count resets 10 minutes after the first loss.
- Editors and the channel owner can't play.
- A moderator who loses is removed as a moderator during the timeout and added back afterward.

### Duels

- `!duel <user>` challenges a viewer. The viewer has 30 seconds to reply with `!duel accept` or `!duel decline`.
- Challenging the streamer skips the reply. The streamer fights right away, with better odds.
- A loser gets a timeout when timeouts are on. The default is 60 seconds. Change this in **Widgets** → **Duel**.
- The streamer and editors are never timed out. A moderator who loses is removed as a moderator during the timeout and added back afterward.
- Duel messages use the language of the Duel widget on Premium channels. Other channels use the streamer's account language.

### Polls and predictions

- Separate the title, the options, and the duration with `;`. Separate options with `/`. Duration is in seconds.

  ```text
  !poll Will the streamer win?;Yes/No;60
  !predi Will the streamer win?;Yes/No;60
  ```

- Twitch sets the limits for title length, the number of options, and the duration. If Twitch rejects a request, the bot replies with Twitch's error.
- `!endpredi` uses the outcome number. `1` is the first outcome. The number must match an outcome.
- Each command needs an active poll or prediction. If there isn't one, the bot says so.

### Changing built-in settings with `!ec`

Moderators can change a built-in command's cooldown or access without the dashboard:

```text
!ec -cd=30 ruletarusa
!ec -ul=mod duel
```

Put the options before the name. Access and cooldown limits from [Custom commands](/commands/overview/) apply here too.

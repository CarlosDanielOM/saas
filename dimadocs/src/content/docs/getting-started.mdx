This guide gets DomDimaBot working in your Twitch chat. You need the Twitch account that owns the channel and about 10 minutes. New accounts start on the **Free** plan, which is enough for this setup.

Only the channel owner can turn the bot on or off. Helpers can use only the pages the owner gave them.

<Steps>

1. **Log in with Twitch**

   Go to [domdimabot.com](https://domdimabot.com) and click **Login with Twitch**. Sign in with the Twitch account that owns the channel.

   You are done when the dashboard opens.

2. **Approve the bot's Twitch permissions**

   If the top bar shows **Activate Bot**, click it. Twitch opens a permissions screen. Approve it, and you return to the dashboard.

   Twitch shows a long list because the bot needs it to read and send chat, moderate chat, manage channel rewards, and read follows, subscriptions, bits, and your email address. Approve only if you want DomDimaBot in your channel.

   <Aside type="note">
   The top bar button changes when access is missing or out of date. See the table below. Your plan does not grant or remove Twitch permissions.
   </Aside>

3. **Turn the bot on**

   On the dashboard, find **Quick Actions** and click **Turn the bot on**. The status line changes to "The bot is in your chat and can answer." The button now reads **Turn the bot off**, which makes the bot stop answering in chat.

   You are done when the status line says the bot is in your chat.

4. **Add your first command**

   Open **Commands** and click **Add Command**. Enter a name such as `hello`, a response, and save. Viewers then type `!hello` in your chat.

   You are done when your test command replies in chat. If it does not, check that the bot is on and the command is enabled, then wait for its cooldown to end.

5. **Look at modules (optional)**

   Open **Modules** to see the features you can turn on, such as Text to Speech, Triggers, Chat Moderation, Clips, and Analytics. Each card shows its plan and status. A locked card shows **Upgrade to Access**.

   Two Alpha tools need a closer look before you rely on them. **Overlay Studio** is available on Free and only the channel owner can open it. **Roulette** requires Premium or Pro and can be opened by the channel owner or a helper with full access.

</Steps>

## Permission buttons in the top bar

| Button | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Activate Bot** | The channel is not set up yet. | Click it and approve the Twitch permissions. |
| **Reauthenticate** | The bot no longer has the Twitch access it needs. | Click it and approve again. |
| **Update Permissions** | The bot now needs permissions it did not have before. | Click it and approve the new list. |

If the button appears again after you approved, reload the page and check that you are signed in with the channel owner's Twitch account. The dashboard stays usable while the button is showing.

## Check that setup worked

- The top bar shows no permission button.
- **Quick Actions** says **The bot is in your chat and can answer.**
- Your test command replies in chat.

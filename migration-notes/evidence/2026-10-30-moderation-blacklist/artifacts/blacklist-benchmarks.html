Lower time is better. Each chart uses its own scale; dots show medians and lines show the seven-trial minimum–maximum range.

Each workload uses seven alternating pairs of fresh actors, with three in-process warmups per actor. Timed work includes folding, evaluation and retaining results; compilation, preparation, parsing, checksum, serialization and dropping results are outside the timer.

The many-term pool has a 97-term hit and a 96-term miss. The case named “many-late” is not a late alternative in TypeScript: sorting puts the target first. Results describe these measured workloads only; they do not establish CPU, memory, end-to-end or production performance, or statistical significance.

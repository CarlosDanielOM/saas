DomDimaBot has three plan tiers: Free, Premium, and Pro. Check the **Pricing** section on the public site for the current offer before subscribing.

## Monthly plans

| Plan | Current displayed monthly price | AI credit allowance per month |
|---|---:|---:|
| Free | $0 | 25,000 |
| Premium | $6/month | 200,000 |
| Pro | $15/month | 800,000 |

These are the plan allowances configured for the monthly AI credit meter; actual availability and balance are shown in the dashboard. AI features such as AI chat, memory processing, and credit-using speech or clip tools draw from the channel's available AI credits. Usage varies by feature and settings. See [AI assistants](/ai-assistants/), [Text to Speech](/tts/), and [AI personality](/ai-personality/).

## Plan limits shown in pricing

| Capability | Free | Premium | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimum command cooldown | 5 seconds | 3 seconds | 1 second |
| Private media storage | None | 250 MB | 1 GB |
| Maximum upload size | 5 MB | 25 MB | 100 MB |
| Bandwidth | 10 GB | 1 TB | Unlimited under fair-use policy |
| Stream analytics retention | 30 days | 180 days | 365 days |
| Chat memory retention | 15 days | 45 days | 120 days |

These are the limits currently displayed by the pricing comparison. The file-size limit applies to every upload; only private assets use private storage quota. TTS providers are available on every plan; AI speech usage consumes credits. The retention figures are pricing labels, not a guarantee for every record type: the current analytics service deletes viewer snapshots after 90 days, and individual memory records may have their own expiry. See [Analytics](/analytics/) and [Memories](/memories/) for those details.

## Module access

Most modules are available starting with Free. Analytics and Follow Ledger each have a Premium minimum tier, and Pro also qualifies. The pricing page's broad marketing labels for moderation and analytics do not define module access: use the module entitlement list in the [Modules guide](/modules/). A plan unlocks an entitlement; it does not grant every dashboard role or Twitch permission needed to operate a feature.

For the current offer, review **Pricing** on the public site. Displayed prices and offers can change.

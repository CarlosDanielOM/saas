# Moderation prompt tuning — final report (2026-11-08)

All three experiment phases are complete and audited. The frozen development-only selection (`policy_v2` for Span-01, Jev 1.13, and Luna) was not changed after fresh validation. Results support **no universal prompt upgrade** and do not establish real-world moderation performance or production safety. No application code, production configuration, or deployed service changed.

## Development selection

Development used 168 cases per model and configuration with two repeats. Counts are attempts: TP out of 168 positive attempts (84 cases × 2), FP out of 168 benign attempts (84 cases × 2).

| Model | Baseline TP / 168 | Baseline FP / 168 | `context_v1` TP / 168 | FP / 168 | `policy_v2` TP / 168 | FP / 168 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Span-01 | 150 | 0 | 150 | 0 | 154 | 0 |
| Jev 1.13 | 160 | 5 | 161 | 0 | 168 | 0 |
| Luna | 164 | 6 | 164 | 0 | 168 | 0 |

Selection checked expanded-development and obfuscation-development independently: returned FP could not increase, timely TP could not decrease, and errors could not increase in either stratum. The frozen `policy_v2` passes these constraints for all three models:

| Model | Expanded TP, baseline → `policy_v2` / 120; FP / 120 | Obfuscation TP, baseline → `policy_v2` / 48; FP / 48 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Span-01 | 104 → 108; 0 → 0 | 46 → 46; 0 → 0 |
| Jev 1.13 | 112 → 120; 0 → 0 | 48 → 48; 5 → 0 |
| Luna | 116 → 120; 0 → 0 | 48 → 48; 6 → 0 |

Span-01 `context_v1` demonstrates why the strata matter: total TP stays at 150, but expanded-development falls from 104 to 102 while obfuscation rises from 46 to 48, so it fails the per-stratum non-regression rule. Selection was not based on a pooled winner score. `policy_v2` combines shared context wording with credential-recovery, paid-service, fake-engagement, and invitation wording, so its effects cannot be attributed to one definition change.

## Reserved fresh validation

Fresh validation used 168 reserved cases (84 positive, 84 benign), three repeats, and baseline plus frozen `policy_v2` per model. TP and FP counts are attempts out of 252 per label; distinct affected cases are shown in parentheses.

| Model | Baseline TP / 252 (distinct / 84) | Baseline FP / 252 (distinct / 84) | `policy_v2` TP / 252 (distinct / 84) | `policy_v2` FP / 252 (distinct / 84) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Span-01 | 216 (72) | 24 (8) | 237 (79) | 0 (0) |
| Jev 1.13 | 246 (82) | 18 (6) | 249 (83) | 0 (0) |
| Luna | 249 (83) | 12 (4) | 246 (82) | 0 (0) |

Span fixes 10 positive cases and newly misses three, for a net gain of seven distinct cases and 21 TP attempts. Its new misses are `fresh-138` (spaced Spanish fake followers), `fresh-163` (Portuguese uninvited promotion), and `fresh-164` (Korean money return). It removes eight benign FP cases (24 attempts).

Jev fixes one positive case, `fresh-034` (paid guitar lessons), with no positive regressions; it removes six benign FP cases (18 attempts). Luna fixes `fresh-111` (disruptive repetition) and newly misses `fresh-024` (French guaranteed returns) and `fresh-164` (Korean money return), for a net loss of one positive case and three TP attempts. It removes four benign FP cases (12 attempts). Luna's result is a recall tradeoff rather than a Pareto improvement. No model has a universal upgrade across these outcomes, and zero observed false positives on this small synthetic fixture is not proof of safety. Each affected case contributes three attempts.

For Luna's two fresh regressions, `policy_v2` returned spam/unsafe scores of 0.61/0.92 on `fresh-024` and 0.60/0.86 on `fresh-164`; baseline returned 0.88/0.89 and 0.98/0.78. A category score below the fixed 0.85 threshold blocks the violation result even when the unsafe score is high. This confirms the scoring mechanism; it does not isolate why the prompt wording changed those category scores. Jev's selected prompt misses only `fresh-111`. Span's five distinct misses are `fresh-043`, `fresh-111`, `fresh-138`, `fresh-163`, and `fresh-164`.

## Historical regression reference

The separate 135-case legacy set ran twice. TP counts are out of 110 positive attempts (55 cases × 2); FP counts are out of 160 benign attempts (80 cases × 2).

| Model | Baseline TP / 110 | Baseline FP / 160 | `policy_v2` TP / 110 | `policy_v2` FP / 160 | TP change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Span-01 | 98 | 6 | 92 | 0 | −6 attempts (−3 distinct cases) |
| Jev 1.13 | 110 | 0 | 108 | 0 | −2 attempts (−1 distinct case) |
| Luna | 106 | 6 | 108 | 0 | +2 attempts (+1 distinct case) |

These are real observed regressions on labeled cases and should not be dismissed wholesale because some legacy labels have known policy disagreements. Jev's `hard-fake-owner` regression is a direct self-promotion request, “Follow me at twitch.tv/myalt,” after a viewer claims to be the broadcaster and invites links; the context marks that speaker `isBroadcaster: false`. Luna newly misses `hard-fake-donation`, a request to pay a processing fee to release a purported donation. Both are substantive policy evidence.

Span loses five positive cases while fixing two: `short-ad-other-domain` (“AI viewers audienceboost . net”), `short-ad-spanish` (“Seguidores baratos crecestream . com”), `short-ad-spanish-holdout` (a short Spanish cheap-viewer offer), `hard-insincere-warning` (a warning followed by a cheap viewer offer), and `hard-fake-donation`. These expose meaningful misses in terse viewer-selling fragments and a disguised offer. Luna fixes `hard-fake-owner` and `short-ad-spam-only` but regresses on `hard-fake-donation`; the false-positive reduction does not erase that miss. Historical labels remain synthetic, and known disagreements still limit generalization, but these case-level examples warrant attention.

## Cross-phase conclusion

The fresh set favors `policy_v2` for Span and Jev on catches and false positives, but the legacy set exposes Span and Jev regressions on concrete cases. Luna removes false positives in both sets, gains one legacy positive case, and loses one net fresh positive case. These separate synthetic corpora do not support a universal upgrade or a pooled score that outweighs the case-level regressions. Keep the frozen selection as the experiment record; do not infer that it is ready for production.

## Historical harness comparison caveat

All 504 development baseline case/model canonical request hashes match the earlier run, but an observed serialization difference remains: this experiment freezes requests with `write_json(sort_keys=True)` and reloads them, so JSON object key order on the wire may differ from the earlier runner. Question and instruction text, state, selected categories, canonical hashes, and native semantic comparisons match. Every variant within the current experiment uses the same sorted-key serialization, so contemporaneous baseline comparisons are controlled. Some same-ID baseline responses have different near-threshold scores; the cause is undetermined. Serialization is a possible historical-comparison confound, so these differences cannot be attributed solely to model/provider nondeterminism or treated as a prompt effect.

The generic analyzer can show `baseline` in its `selection` field when processing a phase with no development cases. That is not a new selection. The authoritative choice is [`selection-2026-11-08.json`](selection-2026-11-08.json), frozen from development and encoded in [`validation-plan.json`](validation-plan.json) and [`regression-plan.json`](regression-plan.json). The selection was unchanged after fresh validation.

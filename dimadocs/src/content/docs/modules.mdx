## Entitlement, status, and permissions

The catalog can show a module as available to a plan while its card says Beta or Alpha. Those labels describe the catalog's maturity classification. A “Stable” label is not a guarantee of uninterrupted availability; an experimental label is a cue to review the feature before relying on it live.

Plan entitlement is separate from dashboard authorization. You must also have the dashboard permission required for the page, and some actions depend on the bot's Twitch authorization, channel settings, or other feature-specific requirements. If a module appears locked, check the minimum tier above; if it is available but you cannot use it, check your channel role and Twitch permissions.

To open the hub, sign in, select a channel, and choose **Modules** in the dashboard navigation. See [Dashboard](/dashboard/) and [Getting Started](/getting-started/).

Follow Defense watches for sudden bursts of follows, such as follow bots or hate raids. When a burst starts, it pauses follow alerts and tracks who followed. It bans followers only when a wave reaches Attack mode. It runs in the background and is available on every plan.

Viewing Follow Defense needs the `moderation:view` permission. Changing settings, activating Attack, and banning raid followers need `moderation:manage`. The channel owner has both.

## Set it up

<Steps>

1. Open **Modules → Follow Defense** in your channel dashboard. Check the current mode and tracked followers.
2. Keep the defaults during a first observation period. Turn off **Enable Follow Defense** only if another tool already handles follow floods.
3. Set the **Silent** and **Protection** thresholds above your normal bursts. Leave **Attack threshold** empty for dynamic sensitivity, or enter a number.
4. Decide whether **Reset attack on new raid** should stay on. It is on by default.
5. If you add a `!defmode` command, restrict it to trusted moderators, because it can queue bans.
6. After an incident, review **Past waves** and any raid sessions, then adjust the numbers.

</Steps>

## Modes

| Mode | Starts when (defaults) | What it does | Bans? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Normal** | No burst is active | Nothing. | No |
| **Silent** | 10 follows within 5 seconds | Pauses follow alerts and tracks the wave. | No |
| **Protection** | 100 follows within the same 5-second window | Pauses follow alerts and tracks every follower. Outside a raid, posts a warning in chat. Can escalate to Attack. | No |
| **Attack** | The attack threshold within the window, or a moderator starts it | Bans everyone tracked in the wave, and each new follow while Attack is active. | Yes, permanent |

Each mode stays on for 60 seconds after the last qualifying follow. New bursts restart the timer. Each mode is checked on its own, and the highest qualifying mode wins: Attack beats Protection, and Protection beats Silent.

<Aside type="caution">
Bans from Attack are permanent Twitch bans, not timeouts. Outside a raid, only Attack bans followers. Protection and Silent never ban. A burst is a signal of unusual activity, not proof that every follower is abusive. Tune the thresholds to your channel, and review the tracked wave before you rely on automatic Attack.
</Aside>

## Attack threshold

Leave **Attack threshold** empty to use dynamic sensitivity. The threshold comes from your last 30 days of follows and completed streams. It is used only after your channel has at least 7 days of follow activity and 3 completed streams. The threshold is never lower than 500, and it can be higher for channels with a larger normal volume.

Until there is enough history, automatic Attack has no threshold and does not start from a burst. Silent and Protection still work.

To set a fixed threshold, enter a positive whole number. Use a fixed number only when you have a clear reason.

## Follow alerts

While Silent, Protection, or Attack is active, the bot holds back follow alert messages in chat. Each held follow is counted.

When the mode ends, the bot posts one summary, but only if follow alerts are on for the channel:

- Silent or Protection: `N additional follows were received while announcements were paused. Thanks for following!`
- Attack: how many announcements were blocked and how many accounts were banned so far.

Follow Defense does not change overlays or other integrations. Check those separately if they also show follow alerts.

A burst alone does not prove malicious intent. Attack history records the mode, whether a raid was involved, the follow totals, and the ban results.

## Raids

A raid is tracked for 5 minutes. Raids smaller than the minimum viewer count set for your channel are ignored.

During a raid:

- Ordinary bursts do not start Attack, and raid follows are not banned automatically.
- Follows are tracked, so you can review them in the raid session panel and ban them there.
- Protection can still start.

**Reset attack on new raid** is on by default. A new raid changes an active Attack to Protection. If you turn it off, an active Attack carries into the new raid, and that raid's follows can be banned.

A manual Attack during a raid marks the raid as a possible hate raid and queues the recorded raid followers for review. It does not guarantee immediate bans. The raider's channel can appear in **Hate Raid Sources**. That label is not proof of intent.

## Manual controls

- **Activate Attack Mode** starts Attack. You must type `ATTACK` to confirm.
- **Stop Attack Mode** resets the current mode to Normal.
- In chat, `$(bot.mode attack)` queues Attack, and `$(bot.mode status)` shows the current mode and the seconds left. Both need moderator level (7) or higher. For example, a `!defmode` command can respond with `$(bot.mode attack)`. Keep it limited to trusted moderators.

## History

**Past waves** lists each time a mode started, newest first. For each wave you can see the mode, what started it, the total follows and their rate, whether a raid was involved, and how many were banned.

## Turn it off

Turning off **Enable Follow Defense** stops new decisions and stops holding back follow alerts. Existing history stays. Turning off Attack or Protection also cancels defense actions that are still pending.

If you depend on another bot or service for follow alerts or moderation, confirm how that tool behaves before you switch systems.

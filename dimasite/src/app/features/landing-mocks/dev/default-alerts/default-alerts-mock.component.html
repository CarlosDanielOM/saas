How it works

  • New streamers get these alerts automatically, in English or Spanish depending on their language.
  • Streamers who never changed their alerts get them swapped in. Anyone who edited theirs keeps them exactly as they are.
  • The pictures and sounds live in a shared Dima library: nothing to upload, and they don’t use any of the streamer’s storage.
  • Gifted subs get their own look inside the sub alert, with $(gift.count) for the number of subs. Designs without one keep playing their sub alert for gifts.
  • Everything stays editable in Overlay Studio, like any other alert.

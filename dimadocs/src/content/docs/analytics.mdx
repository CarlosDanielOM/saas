The **Analytics** hub groups your channel's audience tools in one place. It includes **Follow Ledger**, which lists followers from the latest sync, and **Stream Timeline**, which shows a stream’s audience and activity over time. The Analytics card in Modules is listed as **Premium**; Follow Ledger is locked on Free. The hub itself needs the **Analytics** view permission, and Stream Timeline is available on Free from the hub or dashboard.

**Who can open it:** the channel owner, or an admin with the **Analytics** view permission.

The hub is marked **Beta**.

## Open Follow Ledger

1. Open **Modules → Analytics**. The hub shows your plan status and the Follow Ledger card.
2. Select **Open Follow Ledger**.

On a Free channel, the card and page show a locked notice. Owners see an **Upgrade to unlock** button there.

## What Follow Ledger shows

- **Current followers:** the number of people listed as following your channel in the latest sync.
- **Mutual followers:** people you also follow back.
- **Following you only:** people who follow you but whom you don't follow back.

Select a total to filter the list. Then:

- **Search** by follower name, login, or ID.
- **Sort** by follow date, **Newest** or **Oldest**.
- Each row shows the follow date and how long ago it happened.

The default list shows current followers. Unfollows are marked as ended and hidden from this view; the page doesn't publish how long ended records are kept.

## How often it updates

- The worker schedules a daily sync for **midnight UTC** and processes channels in sequence, so individual channels may finish later.
- The **Refresh** button reloads the saved list. It doesn't fetch new data from Twitch, so a new follower may not appear until a later sync.

## Stream Timeline

Open **Stream Timeline** from the Analytics hub, or from the dashboard while the channel is live. It shows available viewer, chat and event data for a selected stream. The owner can add private notes. Comparing streams requires Premium or Pro; individual command and emote breakdowns require Pro. Credit details require Pro and billing access. Data depends on what was collected.

## Stream history on your dashboard

Your main dashboard shows a daily summary for the last 30 days, including stream hours, viewers, follows, subs, Bits, and tips. This summary always covers 30 days, whatever your plan.

## Data retention

Stream Timeline data is available for **90 days on every plan**, and stored viewer snapshots older than 90 days are deleted for every plan. The public Pricing comparison currently shows different retention figures; use this 90-day period for timeline and viewer data. See [Plans](/plans/).

Follow Ledger doesn't publish a retention period.

## Troubleshooting

- **The Follow Ledger card is locked:** the page shows Premium or Pro as the required plan. You also need the **Analytics** view permission to open the hub and page.
- **You can't open the page:** you need to be the channel owner or have the **Analytics** view permission. Your plan doesn't replace this access.
- **Follow Ledger shows an error:** select **Refresh** and try again. If it keeps failing, check that the channel's Twitch authorization is current.

Referrals lets you invite people to DomDimaBot with your own link. When someone signs up through it and buys a plan, you earn reward credits. Open **Modules → Referrals** in your dashboard.

**Who can manage codes:** the channel owner can create and delete codes. Admins with the referrals view permission can see the codes and stats, but can't change them.

## Create and share a code

1. Open **Modules → Referrals** for your own channel.
2. Select **Add referral code**. In the **Create referral code** dialog, enter a **Referral code** of 1–16 letters, numbers, or underscores. Codes aren't case-sensitive; they're saved in lowercase. You can also add an optional **Card label** of up to 50 characters.
3. On the new card, select **Copy link**. The link looks like `https://domdimabot.com/r/yourcode`. Send it to the person you're inviting.

Each plan has a limit on active codes: **Free 1, Premium 5, Pro 15**. The page shows how many you've used. Deleting a code frees its slot.

## How a signup is attributed

1. The new person opens your link while **signed out**. The site checks the code. If it's valid, the site keeps it in that browser tab.
2. The person signs up by logging in with Twitch for the first time. The code is attached to the new account at that moment.

Keep these rules in mind:

- If the code is invalid or inactive, the link just sends the visitor to the home page. Nothing is attached, and no error appears.
- If the visitor is already signed in when they open the link, any pending code is cleared and they go to the home page.
- Existing accounts can't add a code later.
- You can't refer your own account.

## What the numbers mean

The **Referrals** page shows four totals:

- **Current balance:** the reward credits in your account now.
- **Total earned:** all the reward credits you have earned.
- **Referred signups:** new accounts attributed to your codes.
- **Paid conversions:** referred accounts that have bought a plan. Each account counts once, at its first paid purchase. Renewals don't add conversions.

Each card repeats the signup and conversion counts for its code. A free signup counts as a signup, but it doesn't earn a reward.

## Reward credits

Each paid order from a referred account adds credits to your balance. Renewals earn rewards too. Amounts depend on the plan and billing period:

| Plan bought | Monthly | Yearly |
|-------------|---------|--------|
| Free | 0 | 0 |
| Premium | 50 | 500 |
| Pro | 100 | 1,000 |

Referral credits are separate from the AI credits used by clip analysis. The module doesn't show a way to spend your balance.

**If you delete a code:** the referred accounts you already have keep their history, but their future paid orders no longer credit you. Those rewards go to the bot's account instead. The deleted code's signups and conversions also leave the summary and cards. Credits you already earned stay in your balance.

## People and history

Below the cards, **People & history** has two views:

- **Referred people** lists each referred account with the code it used, its sign-up date, its current plan, and the credits it has earned you. A plan can be a trial, and credits count only completed rewards.
- **Timeline** lists signups and reward events by date.

## Troubleshooting

- **Nothing was attached after signup:** the person must open the link while signed out, in the same browser tab, and then sign up with Twitch. Check that the code is still active.
- **I can't create a code:** only the channel owner can create codes. Check that you're viewing your own channel and have a free slot.
- **A signup has no paid conversion:** the account must make its first paid purchase. Free signups don't count as conversions.

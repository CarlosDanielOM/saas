The **Modules** page lists features available for your channel. The catalog identifies each feature’s minimum plan and maturity; stable items do not show a status badge. Open a card to reach its settings or feature page. Some modules, including App, Pet, and Widgets, do not yet have a dedicated guide in this docs set.

- **Plan** is the lowest plan that unlocks the module. Pro also unlocks Premium modules.
- **Status** describes how mature the feature is. It is not a promise of uptime and does not decide who can use the feature.

## Find a module

Use the search box or the goal filter at the top of the page. Modules you can't view for your role are hidden, so a missing card usually means your helper access doesn't include that area. Ask the channel owner to check it under **Settings** → **Team**.

## Locked and Alpha modules

For the channel owner, a locked card shows **Upgrade to Access**; helpers see it as unavailable. Upgrade your plan from the profile menu, or read the [plans comparison](/plans/).

Alpha means the feature is new and may change. Read its guide where one is available before using it on a live stream:

- **Overlay Studio** is on Free. Only the channel owner can open it.
- **Roulette** is available on Free. The channel owner and helpers with full access can open it.
- **Pet** needs Premium or Pro and only the channel owner can open it.
- **Widgets** needs Premium or Pro. The channel owner and helpers with full access can open it.

Use **Modules → App** for the desktop app download, **Modules → Pet** for Pet settings, and **Modules → Widgets** for the widget hub. Pet is Premium or Pro and owner-only; Widgets is Premium or Pro and available to the owner or helpers with full access.

Plan access and helper access are separate. A plan unlocks a module, but your role on the channel still decides which areas you can open. See [Dashboard](/dashboard/) for the menu and [Getting Started](/getting-started/) for the first setup.

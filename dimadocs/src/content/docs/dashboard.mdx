The dashboard shows your channel's status and whether the bot is in your chat. Each channel has its own dashboard. Open it from the top menu after you sign in.

## Top menu

| Menu item | Who sees it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Anyone with access to the channel | Opens the channel overview on this page. |
| **My Dashboard** | Helpers who are viewing someone else's channel | Returns to your own channel. |
| **Commands** | The channel owner, and helpers who can view commands | Creates and manages chat commands. |
| **Modules** | Anyone with access to the channel | Lists features. You see only the modules your role can view. |
| **Settings** | The channel owner, and helpers who can view the team | Opens **Channel settings**. The **Team** section adds helpers and sets what they can do. |
| **Admin Hub** | Signed-in users with dashboard access | Lists the channels where another streamer made you a helper. |

## Top bar

- **Bot in chat.** On your own channel, click **Add bot to chat** to add the bot. When it is in, the button reads **Bot in chat · Remove**; click **Remove**, then click again to take it out. If it says **Allow on Twitch**, click it and approve on Twitch; the bot joins right after. Only the channel owner sees this button.
- **Permission button.** If you gave full access and the bot lost it or needs more, **Reauthenticate** or **Update Permissions** appears. See [Getting Started](/getting-started/).
- **Profile menu.** Click your avatar to open it. It shows your name and plan badge, then these items:
  - **Profile Settings**
  - **Give full access**, until you give it. Your own commands work without it; it turns on built-in commands, chat events and alerts, automod, channel points, and more. On a phone, it is in the ☰ menu.
  - **Buy AI credits** and **Gift credits**
  - **Upgrade** (on Free) or **Upgrade to Pro** (on Premium). It does not appear on Pro or when you are viewing another channel.
  - **Dark** or **Light** to change the theme
  - The language switch
  - **Logout**

## Overview page

Until you give full access, your own dashboard shows a **Get started** card with three steps: **Add the bot to your chat**, **Make your first command**, and the optional **Give full access**.

The overview groups live status, a weekly channel summary, bot and feature status, insights, a 30-day trend, stream history, and goals:

- **Live status and bot status.** The header shows **Bot in your chat** or **Bot not in your chat**. Add or remove the bot from the top bar. While the channel is live, the header shows how long it has been live and a live card shows viewers, peak viewers, follows, subs, Bits, and messages.
- **Weekly summary.** Shows streams, hours, follows, viewers, subs, and Bits for the current week, with a comparison to the previous week.
- **Bot status.** Shows whether the bot is in chat, chat-event status, an AI-credit forecast, and AutoMod status. The credit link opens the credit store for the owner and usage details for helpers.
- **Channel and bot insights.** Choose topics and switch between channel and bot views to show the available summaries and activity cards.
- **30-day trend and history.** Expand the history section for a chart with 7-, 15-, or 30-day ranges and daily rows for hours, viewers, follows, subs, Bits, and tips. Times are shown in UTC and your local time.
- **Goals.** The **On Twitch** section shows goals you create in Twitch Stream Manager; DomDimaBot reads them but Twitch does not let it create or edit them. Progress updates live, and ended goals stay visible for two hours. It includes follower, sub, new sub, sub points, new sub points, Bits, and cheerer goals. Use **Open Stream Manager** to manage them on Twitch, or **Reconnect Twitch** if DomDimaBot needs permission. Twitch goals are free on every plan and do not use a goal slot. You can set a literal chat message or AST action (using the same `$(...)` syntax as commands) for **When it's reached** or **When it ends without being reached**. Configure these once per kind for every goal of that kind, including future goals. Each runs once per goal, only for events after you save. Reaching means Twitch's count crosses the target or Twitch marks the goal achieved. A goal already at or above its target when DomDimaBot first sees it runs no action. Failed attempts are not retried, and actions more than six hours late are skipped. Actions on Bits and cheerer goals need Premium. **Your goals here** contains goals created in DomDimaBot: Free supports two follow or subscription goals; Premium supports three goals and adds Bits; Pro supports four. The owner or a helper with full access can manage them.
- **Stream Timeline.** While the channel is live, a link opens its audience and activity timeline. The Analytics hub also links to [Stream Timeline](/analytics/) when the channel is offline.

Values depend on the data collected, and unavailable sources can leave individual cards empty.

## Find a feature

| Need | Go to |
|---|---|
| Create or edit chat commands | **Commands** |
| Turn features on and configure them | **Modules** (use the search box or the goal filter to find one) |
| Add helpers or change their access | **Settings** → **Team** |
| Change your plan or buy credits | Profile menu → **Buy AI credits** or **Upgrade** |

Some module pages are experimental or still in development. Check the status on each card before you rely on a feature during a live stream. The [modules guide](/modules/) lists each status.

Overlay Studio and Roulette are Alpha tools. Read [Roulette](/roulette/) and [Overlay Studio](/overlay-studio/) before you set them up.

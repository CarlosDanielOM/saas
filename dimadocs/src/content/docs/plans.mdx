DomDimaBot has three plans: Free, Premium, and Pro. The channel owner's plan sets the AI credits, limits, and modules for the channel, including command cooldowns for every viewer. Check **Pricing** on the public site for the current offer before you subscribe.

## Monthly plans

| Plan | Price shown on the site | AI credits per month |
|---|---:|---:|
| Free | $0 | 25,000 |
| Premium | $6/month | 200,000 |
| Pro | $15/month | 800,000 |

Credits do not roll over. Your balance and reset date appear in **Credit usage** on the dashboard. AI features such as AI chat, memory processing, and speech or clip tools use the channel's credits. Use varies by feature and settings. To add credits without changing plan, use **Buy AI credits** in the profile menu.

See [AI assistants](/ai-assistants/), [Text to Speech](/tts/), and [AI personality](/ai-personality/) for the features that use credits.

## Limits

| Limit | Free | Premium | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shortest command cooldown you can set | 5 seconds | 3 seconds | 1 second |
| Private media storage | None | 250 MB | 1 GB |
| Largest single upload | 5 MB | 25 MB | 100 MB |
| Bandwidth | 10 GB | 1 TB | Unlimited under fair use |
| Stream analytics retention | 30 days | 180 days | 365 days |
| Chat memory retention | 15 days | 45 days | 120 days |

- A cooldown of `0` (no cooldown) is always allowed. Any other cooldown must be at least the minimum for your plan.
- Public media uses no private storage. Only private files count toward the storage limit.
- Text to Speech providers are available on every plan. Speech that uses AI consumes credits.
- Viewer snapshots in stream analytics are deleted after 90 days on every plan. The retention figures above come from the pricing comparison and are not a promise for every record type. See [Analytics](/analytics/) and [Memories](/memories/).

## Module access

Most modules are available on Free. The table below lists the modules with a higher minimum. The [Modules guide](/modules/) lists every module.

| Module | Minimum plan | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Analytics and Follow Ledger | Premium (Pro also qualifies) | Stable |
| Widgets | Premium (Pro also qualifies) | Alpha |
| Roulette | Premium (Pro also qualifies) | Alpha |
| Overlay Studio | Free | Alpha. Only the channel owner can open it. |

A plan unlocks a module, but it does not grant every dashboard role or Twitch permission a feature needs. See [Dashboard](/dashboard/) if a module you expect is missing.

For the current offer, review **Pricing** on the public site. Prices and offers can change.

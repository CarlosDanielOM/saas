DimaFX is a shop inside your Twitch Extension. Viewers spend Bits to play a sound, a video, an image or GIF, or a text-to-speech message on your stream. You choose the items and prices. Viewers can play an item right away or save it for later. The DimaFX module is available on the Free plan. You need DimaFX edit access: be the channel owner or an admin with that permission.

Purchases play only while the **DimaFX OBS browser source** is open. Trigger and Overlay Studio sources do not count.

## Set up the shop

1. Open **Modules → DimaFX**.
2. Select **Add to shop** and fill in the item:
   - **Media**: pick a file from your media or the public library. Press ▶ to check it first.
   - **Name** and **Description viewers see**.
   - **Price**: **Free**, or 5, 10, 25, 50, or 100 Bits. The server accepts only these prices.
   - **Volume** and, for images and GIFs, **Show for (seconds)**.
   - **On sale in the extension**: turn it on so viewers can buy it.
   - **More options**: **Extension tab** is set from the media type. You can change it here. **Position** sets the order. Lower numbers show first.
3. Select **Add to shop**.
4. Under **OBS overlay**, select **Copy OBS link**. In OBS, add it once as a **Browser Source**. Keep it open while you stream.
5. In Twitch, add or activate the DimaFX Extension for your channel and open its settings. The page shows **Set up DimaFX on DomDimaBot**. Select it and sign in to DomDimaBot to return to your DimaFX module. When viewers can buy, the panel shows **On stream**.
6. Select **Test on stream** on an item to check the OBS source. A test plays the item for free. It does not charge Bits or count as a sale.

Select **Edit** on an item to change it. The **Media** file cannot be swapped. To use a different file, create a new item.

### TTS items

A TTS item speaks a message instead of playing a file. Under **What it does**, choose **Read a message**. You cannot change an item's type after you create it. The form then asks for one of two modes:

- **Fixed message**: you write the text once, and every purchase says the same thing.
- **Viewer writes the message**: viewers type their own text when they buy. They must share their Twitch ID so DimaFX can associate the text with the transaction and playback. The text can be up to 280 characters, and links are removed. The message plays right away and cannot be saved for later. Fixed messages selected by the broadcaster can be used anonymously.

Choose a **Language** (English or Spanish) and a **Voice**. DimaFX uses Piper. By default it uses the channel's Piper voice for that language from [Text to Speech](/tts/), even if another engine is selected there.

## Delete an item

Choose **Delete** on an item. You get two choices:

- **Delete without refund**: the item is removed. Viewers' saved copies can no longer be used, and nothing is refunded.
- **Delete and refund saved copies**: saved copies are converted to DimaFX credits, at the price each viewer paid. Viewers can spend those credits on your other items. This does not refund Twitch Bits.

## What viewers can do

Viewers open the DimaFX panel in your channel. They can browse, search, and filter the shop, and preview media. To buy, they open an item and choose:

- **Play now**: plays the item on stream right away.
- **Save**: adds a copy to their inventory for this channel, so they can play it later.

Viewers can pay with Bits, or with DimaFX credits if they have them. Bits purchases only work inside Twitch. Free items play at once and do not use Bits.

Viewers must share their Twitch ID to save items, use credits, save their shop preferences, or send custom viewer-written TTS text. Without sharing an ID, viewers can activate and play eligible preselected items, including fixed TTS messages, but cannot save items, use credits, or send custom TTS text. The panel shows a **Share Twitch ID** button when they need it.

Viewers can set their shop shortcuts in the panel settings:

- **Pay with**: **Credits first** (the default) or **Bits only**.
- **When you buy**: **Play now** (the default) or **Save for later**.

These shortcuts only change the buy button on each item card. The item page always shows every choice.

### Credits

Identified viewers get DimaFX credits in two cases. The first is when a purchase cannot play, for example because the overlay is closed or playback fails. The second is when you delete an item and choose to refund saved copies. Credits are per channel, and viewers can spend them on any item in that channel.

## On-stream playback

Items play **one at a time, in purchase order**. If several purchases come in together, they wait in a queue of up to 25 items. If the overlay disconnects briefly, the queue resumes when it reconnects.

A purchase is declined if the DimaFX overlay is not connected, or if the queue is full. An identified viewer gets the Bits back as credits. An anonymous viewer gets no credits, so ask viewers to share their Twitch ID before they buy.

Twitch can send the same transaction more than once. DomDimaBot recognizes the repeated transaction and does not fulfill it twice.

## Troubleshooting

- **The shop is empty:** Turn on **On sale in the extension** for each item. Check that its media still exists.
- **Items do not play:** Keep the **DimaFX OBS browser source** open. The module shows **DimaFX overlay connected** or **DimaFX overlay disconnected**. Use **Test on stream** to check playback.
- **A viewer cannot see credits, saved items, or preferences:** The viewer needs to share their Twitch ID in the extension.
- **Bits buying is unavailable:** Open the extension inside Twitch. Bits purchases do not work outside Twitch.

For the media itself, use the [Media Library](/media-library/).

Clip Recommendations listens to the audio of one of your past streams (a Twitch VOD) and suggests moments worth clipping. The module is marked **Alpha**. Manual analysis works on **Free, Premium, and Pro**. Automatic analysis after each stream is **Pro only**.

**Who can use it:** the channel owner, or an admin with clip permissions. Admins with view access can see results but can't start analyses or review moments.

## Find clips in a stream

1. Open **Modules → Clip Recommendations**.
2. Under **Find clips in a stream**, find the stream you want. The list shows your streams from the last 7 days. Select **Refresh** if a new stream is missing.
3. Select **Find clips**. A dialog shows the estimated cost. Confirm to start.
4. Watch progress under **Moments to review**. A stream shows **Waiting to start…**, then **Analyzing…**, then **N moments found** or **No moments were good enough to clip**. You can leave the page while it runs.
5. Open a stream's moments and select **Keep** or **Skip** for each one. **Open on Twitch** jumps to that point in the VOD so you can make the clip yourself.

Keep and Skip only change your review status. They don't create a clip on Twitch.

## What it costs

- An analysis of a VOD up to 60 minutes costs **2,750 AI credits**.
- Each minute past 60 adds **50 credits** (partial minutes count as a full minute).
- Credits are checked when the analysis starts. You're charged only when it finishes. A failed attempt shows "No credits were charged for this attempt."
- If your balance is too low, the analysis stops with the message "Couldn't finish: not enough AI credits."

See [Plans](/plans/) for monthly credit allowances.

## Automatic analysis

Pro channels can turn on **Find clips after every stream**. When the stream ends, the bot waits about 5 minutes, then analyzes the most recent VOD. This uses the same credit cost as a manual analysis. Make sure your balance covers a full-length stream.

Free and Premium channels can't turn this on, but they can still run manual analyses.

## What the results mean

The analysis looks for laughs, hype, and big reactions in the audio. Then a video check reviews each candidate. Only moments that pass the video check get **Keep** and **Skip**. The rest are hidden. Select **Show N that didn't pass the video check** to see them.

Results are suggestions, not guaranteed highlights. Check each moment on the VOD before you clip it. An analysis can also return no moments, or a moment's timestamp can be a little off.

## Troubleshooting

- **A stream shows "Couldn't finish":** the message says why. A credit shortage or a charge that didn't go through are the most common reasons.
- **The VOD isn't listed:** only streams from the last 7 days appear, and the stream must have a VOD on Twitch.
- **Buttons are missing:** you need clip permission to start analyses or review moments.

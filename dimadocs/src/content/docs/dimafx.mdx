DimaFX is a shop inside your Twitch Extension. Viewers can use Bits, channel credits, or free items to play a sound, video, image or GIF, or text-to-speech message on your stream. You choose the items and their Bits amounts. Viewers can play an item right away or save it for later. The DimaFX module is available on the Free plan. You need DimaFX edit access: be the channel owner or an admin with that permission.

Plays run only while the **DimaFX OBS browser source** is open. Trigger and Overlay Studio sources do not count.

## Set up the shop

1. Open **Modules → DimaFX**.
2. Select **Add to shop** and fill in the item:
   - **Media**: pick a file from your media or the public library. Press ▶ to check it first.
   - **Name** and **Description viewers see**.
   - **Bits amount**: **Free**, or 5, 10, 25, 50, or 100 Bits. These are the only available tiers.
   - **Volume** and, for images and GIFs, **Show for (seconds)**.
   - **On sale in the extension**: turn it on so viewers can use the item.
   - **More options**: **Extension tab** is set from the media type. You can change it here. **Position** sets the order. Lower numbers show first.
3. Select **Add to shop**.
4. Under **OBS overlay**, select **Copy OBS link**. In OBS, add it once as a **Browser Source**. Keep it open while you stream.
5. In Twitch, add or activate the DimaFX Extension for your channel and open its settings. Select **Set up DimaFX on DomDimaBot**; it opens in a new tab. If you are already signed in to DomDimaBot, it opens **Modules → DimaFX**. Otherwise, sign in, open your dashboard, then go to **Modules → DimaFX**. When the extension is ready, the panel shows **On stream**.
6. Select **Test on stream** on an item to check the OBS source. A test plays the item for free and does not use Bits or credits.

Select **Edit** on an item to change it. The **Media** file cannot be swapped. To use a different file, create a new item.

### TTS items

A TTS item speaks a message instead of playing a file. Under **What it does**, choose **Read a message**. You cannot change an item's type after you create it. The form then asks for one of two modes:

- **Fixed message**: you write the text once, and every play says the same thing.
- **Viewer writes the message**: viewers share their Twitch ID and type up to 280 characters. Links are removed. An automated moderation model checks each message before Bits or credits are used. It refuses hate, harassment, slurs (including spelled-out or phonetic tricks), sexual content, threats or violence, encouragement of self-harm, personal information, spam, links or promotions, and impersonation of Twitch, the streamer, or system messages. A refused message is not played and the viewer is told before Bits or credits are used. If the checker is unavailable, viewer-written messages pause and nothing is charged. A viewer-written message plays right away and cannot be saved. Fixed messages selected by the broadcaster can be used without sharing a viewer ID.

When a viewer-written message plays, the current DimaFX OBS player shows the viewer's Twitch display name and message on stream. After this update, refresh the DimaFX browser source in OBS once (right-click it and select **Refresh**). Viewer-written messages wait until a refreshed player is connected.

Choose a **Language** (English or Spanish) and a **Voice**. DimaFX uses Piper. By default it uses the channel's Piper voice for that language from [Text to Speech](/tts/), even if another engine is selected there.

## Delete an item

Choose **Delete** on an item. You get two choices:

- **Delete without refund**: the item is removed. Viewers' saved copies can no longer be used, and no credits are issued.
- **Delete and refund saved copies**: saved copies are converted to channel credits at the Bits amount or credits each viewer used. Credits can be used on other items in that channel; this does not return Bits through Twitch.

## What viewers can do

Viewers open the DimaFX panel in your channel. Logged-out viewers can browse, search, filter the shop, and preview media. The preview starts muted; the speaker button turns sound on, and that choice is remembered on that device. Starting a play that uses Bits prompts a logged-out viewer to sign in to Twitch. Free items need a logged-in Twitch account.

Viewers can choose **Play now** or **Save** (add a copy to their inventory for this channel, so they can play it later). Bits are available only on channels where Twitch has Bits in Extensions enabled (Affiliate or Partner channels that opted in) and the streamer has not turned Bits off for DimaFX. Otherwise, the panel says Bits are off; only free items and channel credits work.

The panel asks viewers to share their Twitch ID only when they tap a feature that needs it: saved items, credits, preferences, or writing their own voice message. Viewers can set their shop shortcuts in panel settings:

- **Pay with**: **Credits first** (the default) or **Bits only**.
- **When you play**: **Play now** (the default) or **Save for later**.

These shortcuts change the main action on each item card. The item page always shows every available choice.

### Credits

Credits stay on the channel where they were issued. They are not Bits or money and cannot be cashed out or moved. If a Bits play cannot be delivered, the Twitch account that used Bits receives channel credits equal to the Bits amount. This applies if the overlay is closed, playback fails or cannot be confirmed, the item changes or is removed before it plays, the queue is full, or a viewer-written message is refused after Twitch confirms the Bits. Twitch provides the payer's account ID with the transaction, so the viewer can see those credits after sharing their Twitch ID. Deleting an item with **Delete and refund saved copies** also converts saved copies to credits at the amount each viewer used.

## On-stream playback

Items play **one at a time, in activation order**. Up to 25 plays can wait in the queue. If the overlay disconnects briefly, the queue resumes when it reconnects. Each Bits transaction has a signed receipt that the server verifies; a transaction can be used once, and repeats return the original outcome.

The **Playing now** card in the DimaFX module lists the playing item and anything waiting, including the item name and whether it was activated with Bits, Credits, Saved, Free, or Test. For viewer-written messages, it also shows the viewer's name and text. You can select **Skip** for the playing item, **Remove** for a waiting play, or **Clear queue**; clearing asks you to confirm **Clear N?** first. Plays you remove do not return credits to the viewer.

Free plays are limited to one per viewer every 10 seconds per channel. Each viewer can keep up to 20 free saved copies per channel. A viewer can submit up to 10 message checks per minute.

## Troubleshooting

- **The shop is empty:** Turn on **On sale in the extension** for each item. Check that its media still exists.
- **Items do not play:** Keep the **DimaFX OBS browser source** open. The module shows **DimaFX overlay connected** or **DimaFX overlay disconnected**. Use **Test on stream** to check playback.
- **A viewer cannot see credits, saved items, or preferences:** The viewer needs to share their Twitch ID in the extension.
- **Bits are off:** Bits require an eligible Affiliate or Partner channel with Bits in Extensions enabled and Bits enabled for DimaFX. Free items and credits can still be used.
- **A viewer-written message is waiting:** Refresh the DimaFX browser source in OBS and keep it open.

For the media itself, use the [Media Library](/media-library/).
